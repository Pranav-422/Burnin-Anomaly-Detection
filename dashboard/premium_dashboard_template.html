Module A — Lot-Relative Detection

Per-lot Z-score, IQR, and Isolation Forest jointly across all four checkpoints. Flags components that deviate from their own batch, not just the datasheet limit — catching parts that pass the 50µA spec but drift abnormally against their peers.
