rocket_launch The Aerospace Imperative

Why Spacecraft Require Intelligent Burn-In Screening

warning Zero Serviceability in Deep Space

Unlike terrestrial electronics, a satellite orbiting Earth or en route to deep space cannot be serviced or replaced. A single latent defect in a microcontroller or power PMIC escaping quality control can precipitate catastrophic loss of a multi-crore national space mission.

speed The Blind Spot of Static Thresholds

Traditional screening applies rigid datasheet limits (e.g. max 50µA leakage). A chip that drifts rapidly from 10µA to 48µA still "passes" static gates despite severe latent degradation. Conversely, healthy chips from high-baseline manufacturing lots get wrongly scrapped.

System Architecture

The Three Pillars of ASTROLAB

Replacing static pass/fail testing with an integrated statistical, predictive, and explainable ML framework.

radar

Module A: Dynamic Lot-Relative Anomaly Detection

Evaluates each chip relative to its manufacturing lot rather than global static limits. Combines directional Interquartile Range (IQR k=2.0), lot Z-scores, and cross-parameter Isolation Forest to spot subtle multi-dimensional outliers.

• Directional IQR (Upper Outliers)
• Lot-level Z-score standardisation
• Joint Cross-Parameter Isolation Forest
timeline

Module B: Time-Series Drift Predictor (T+24h)

XGBoost ML regressor forecasts the 168-hour end-of-test reading using early 0h and 24h readings. Calibrated with a 99.8th percentile safety slope, allowing early rejection of degraded parts at 24 hours and saving 85% in thermal chamber energy.

• Forecast 168h drift from 24h data
• 99.8th percentile safety threshold
• Replaces 7-day test with 24-hr decision
psychology

Explainability Layer: SHAP Decision Audits

Aerospace quality control mandates total explainability. ASTROLAB computes SHAP contribution values for every prediction, producing human-readable explanations that detail which electrical parameter drove the flag and by how much.

• Exact SHAP feature contributions
• Plain-language QA audit text
• Full inspector traceability log
vital_signs Electrical Telemetry

Screened Electrical Parameters

Parameter 01

Leakage Current (I_leak)

Unit: Microamperes (µA)

Measures reverse-bias and dielectric leakage across oxide layers. Exponential degradation under high temperature indicates impending gate-oxide breakdown.

Parameter 02

Quiescent Supply (Iddq)

Unit: Microamperes (µA)

Standby supply current when CMOS gates are quiescent. Uniquely sensitive to bridging faults, gate punch-through, and micro-cracks inside silicon dice.

Parameter 03

Propagation Delay (t_pd)

Unit: Nanoseconds (ns)

Gate switching delay across critical logic paths. Hot carrier injection and negative-bias temperature instability (NBTI) degrade transition times over burn-in duration.

verified

MIL-STD-883K Method 1015 Condition D

High Temperature Operating Life (HTOL) screening at 125°C under continuous dynamic electrical excitation.
